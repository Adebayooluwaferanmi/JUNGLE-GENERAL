# JUNGLE GENERAL — Production Bible

## Canon Status
**CURRENT CONCEPT: REAL HUMAN CHARACTERS / HUMAN-WORLD CINEMATIC DRAMA**

The former animal/jungle allegory is superseded. Owl Doctor, antelope patient/family, beaver scientists, lion leadership and animal-role substitutions are not current canon.

## Mission
Produce JUNGLE GENERAL as a coherent cinematic film in which separately generated AI shots behave as photography from one persistent human story world.

## Creative North Star
**Story first. Continuity always. Generation is production—not improvisation.**

## Non-negotiables
1. Use real human characters.
2. Narrative intent controls visual decisions.
3. Character identity remains stable.
4. Physical geography persists across angles.
5. Every action has a traceable before-state and after-state.
6. Lighting, weather, physical state, wardrobe and props evolve causally.
7. Camera movement serves story information and emotion.
8. Generation must not invent story-critical objects, characters or actions.
9. Approved frames/clips become downstream production references.
10. Superseded allegorical assets cannot be used as current references unless explicitly repurposed and re-approved.

## Canon Hierarchy
1. current real-human story canon
2. locked continuity decision
3. approved human character/environment/prop reference
4. scene specification
5. shot specification
6. generation prompt
7. model interpretation

## Visual Standard
- 9:16 primary format
- cinematic hyperrealism / photorealistic live action
- 4K target where supported
- believable human anatomy, performance, wardrobe, clinical/architectural spaces, materials and physical interactions

## Migration Rule
Do **not** mechanically convert old allegorical roles into human equivalents. Exact human identities, names, wardrobe, blocking and geography must come from confirmed post-transition production material.

## Canon Approval
A generated output becomes canon only after explicit approval.

## Continuity Dimensions
Track human identity, appearance, build, wardrobe, body orientation, expression, carried objects, geography, props, vehicles, weather, wetness, lighting/time, damage, screen direction, action axis and action phase.
