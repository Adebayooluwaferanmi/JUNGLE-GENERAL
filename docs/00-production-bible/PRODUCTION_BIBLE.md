# JUNGLE GENERAL — Production Bible

## Mission
Produce JUNGLE GENERAL as a coherent cinematic film in which separately generated AI shots behave as photography from one persistent story world.

## Creative North Star
**Story first. Continuity always. Generation is production—not improvisation.**

### Non-negotiables
1. Narrative intent controls visual decisions.
2. Character identity remains stable.
3. Physical geography persists across angles.
4. Every action has a traceable before-state and after-state.
5. Lighting, weather, wetness, damage, dirt, wardrobe and props evolve causally.
6. Camera movement serves story information and emotion.
7. Generation must not invent story-critical objects, characters or actions.
8. Approved frames/clips become downstream production references.

## Canon Hierarchy
When instructions conflict:
1. locked story event / approved screenplay
2. locked continuity decision
3. approved character/environment/prop reference
4. scene specification
5. shot specification
6. generation prompt
7. model interpretation

A lower level cannot override a higher one.

## Visual Standard
- Primary format: 9:16 vertical
- Target: cinematic hyperrealism / photorealistic live action
- Target resolution: 4K where supported
- Physical materials, rain, mud, fur/skin, fabric, vehicles, clinical equipment, reflections and practical lighting must remain plausible.

## Canon Approval
A generated output becomes canon only after explicit approval. Attractive unused generations remain candidates or rejects.

## Current Locked Geography — Emergency Ward
Until deliberately revised:
- antelope family/patient: camera-left
- Owl Doctor: camera-right
- Diagnostic Laboratory tunnel: rear
- camera: established side of the 180° axis

These relationships must survive changes in shot size and angle.

## Continuity Dimensions
Track identity, build, wardrobe, body orientation, expression, carried objects, geography, props, vehicles, weather, wetness, lighting/time, damage, screen direction, action axis and action phase.
