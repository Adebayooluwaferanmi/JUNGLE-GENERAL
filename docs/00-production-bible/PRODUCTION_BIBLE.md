## Episode 1
**Title: The Wait**

The story dramatizes the invisible laboratory reasoning, verification and validation that sit behind clinical decisions. Delay is not portrayed simply as inefficiency: in Episode 1, Obinna deliberately spends approximately 20 minutes resolving a questionable laboratory result rather than release unreliable data.

## Episode 1 Clinical Canon
High fever/chills/weakness → Dr. Talon considers malaria vs sepsis → purple-top EDTA specimen → initial FBC appears inconsistent with clinical picture → Obinna checks analyzer maintenance → maintenance/cleaning → approximately 20-minute delay → FBC rerun and comparison → malaria-film microscopy → parasites detected → repeat FBC + microscopy validated/released → communication to Dr. Talon.

**Medical safeguard:** malaria positivity supports malaria but does not automatically exclude bacterial sepsis.
