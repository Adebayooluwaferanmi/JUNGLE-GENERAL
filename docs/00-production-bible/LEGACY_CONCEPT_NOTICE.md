# Legacy Concept Notice

## Status
**SUPERSEDED — NOT PRODUCTION CANON**

JUNGLE GENERAL previously used an animal allegory. That concept included animal-role characters and related visual language.

Examples of superseded material include:
- Owl Doctor
- antelope patient/family
- beaver scientist/laboratory roles
- lion leadership
- paw icons
- “Wildlife Care”
- animal-character blocking and eyelines

## Preservation Policy
Legacy material may be retained as development history, but:
1. it cannot control current prompts;
2. it cannot be cited as current character/environment canon;
3. it cannot be translated mechanically into a human equivalent;
4. any intentionally repurposed design element requires explicit re-approval under the human concept.

## Current Concept
Real human characters in a photorealistic Nigerian medical-drama world.
