# Character Bible — Human Canon

## Scientist Obinna
**Canon ID:** JG-CHAR-OBINNA  
**Role:** Senior Medical Laboratory Scientist  
**Age:** approximately 48–52  
**Nationality/setting:** Nigerian  
**Appearance:** dark skin; salt-and-pepper hair; close grey beard; lean-to-medium build.  
**Wardrobe:** white knee-length laboratory coat over navy scrubs.  
**Performance:** calm, meticulous, quietly authoritative.  
**Professional visual rule:** **no stethoscope.**

### Episode 1 Function
Obinna recognizes that Mr. Okoro's initial FBC does not fit the clinical picture. He investigates analyzer maintenance, performs required maintenance/cleaning, accepts an approximately 20-minute delay, reruns and compares the FBC, proceeds to malaria-film microscopy, detects/correlates the parasite finding, validates/releases the laboratory results and communicates the outcome to Dr. Talon.

### Character Rule
His delay is motivated by professional verification, not incompetence or indifference.

## Dr. Talon
**Canon ID:** JG-CHAR-TALON  
**Role:** Physician  
**Age:** approximately 42–48  
**Nationality/setting:** Nigerian  
**Appearance:** dark brown skin; natural black hair.  
**Wardrobe:** white doctor's coat over dark/navy clothing; stethoscope.  
**Performance:** competent, prestigious and somewhat condescending toward laboratory staff, but **not villainous**.

### Episode 1 Function
Assesses Mr. Okoro and considers malaria vs sepsis. Receives the validated laboratory information from Obinna and retains responsibility for diagnosis and treatment.
