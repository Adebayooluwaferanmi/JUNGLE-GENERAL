# JUNGLE GENERAL — Human Character Bible

**Canon status: ACTIVE / HUMAN VERSION**  
**Episode 1: THE WAIT**

This document governs character identity, role, relationships and performance. Animal-era equivalents are superseded and must not be used as references.

## Scientist Obinna
- **Age:** 48–52
- **Identity:** Nigerian male
- **Appearance:** dark skin; salt-and-pepper hair; close grey beard; lean-to-medium build
- **Wardrobe:** white knee-length laboratory coat over navy scrubs
- **Profession:** senior Medical Laboratory Scientist
- **Performance:** calm, meticulous, observant, professionally accountable
- **Episode 1 function:** recognizes that the initial FBC does not fit the clinical picture; investigates analyzer maintenance; performs necessary maintenance/cleaning; reruns and compares the FBC; progresses malaria-film microscopy; validates laboratory findings; communicates with Dr. Talon.
- **Professional principle:** “The machine produces numbers. We validate results.”
- **Boundary:** Obinna validates laboratory results. He does not replace the physician's diagnosis or treatment decision.

## Dr. Talon
- **Age:** 42–48
- **Identity:** Nigerian female
- **Appearance:** dark brown skin; natural black hair
- **Wardrobe:** white coat over dark/navy clothing; stethoscope
- **Profession:** physician
- **Performance:** competent, prestigious and clinically decisive; hierarchical/condescending toward laboratory staff without being reduced to a caricature or villain
- **Episode 1 function:** assesses Mr. Okoro; considers malaria versus sepsis; owns diagnosis and treatment decisions; receives Obinna's validated laboratory communication.
- **Clinical line:** “I know what I suspect. That is not the same as knowing.”

## Dr. Adewale
- **Age:** 60–65
- **Identity:** Nigerian male
- **Profession:** physician; Medical Director / hospital owner
- **Episode 1 status:** principal canon character but primarily relevant to later episodes
- **Performance:** deceptively friendly; institutional authority
- **Dialogue reservation:** “If e easy…” is reserved for the hierarchy-focused later episode and should not be inserted into Episode 1 without an explicit story revision.

## Mr. Okoro
- **Age:** 52–57
- **Identity:** Nigerian male
- **Role:** Episode 1 patient; husband of Mrs. Okoro; father of Chidi
- **Episode 1 state:** seriously unwell/febrile with high fever, chills and weakness
- **Clinical continuity:** patient condition must evolve causally across arrival, assessment, sampling, waiting and result communication.
- **Important:** Mr. Okoro—not Chidi—is the Episode 1 patient.

## Mrs. Okoro
- **Age:** 47–52
- **Identity:** Nigerian female
- **Role:** Mr. Okoro's wife; Chidi's mother
- **Performance:** anxious and concerned, grounded rather than melodramatic
- **Episode 1 bedside line:** “Doctor… what is happening to him?”

## Chidi Okoro
- **Age:** 18–20
- **Identity:** Nigerian male
- **Role:** biological son of Mr. and Mrs. Okoro
- **Family resemblance:** should plausibly resemble both parents
- **Performance:** protective, impatient and outspoken; may be disrespectful toward the laboratory process while showing more deference toward physicians
- **Episode 1 function:** voices pressure for immediate answers and helps dramatize the public-facing experience of waiting.
- **Important:** any screen or record showing “OKORO, CHIDI” as the patient is non-canonical.

## Relationship Lock
- Mr. Okoro + Mrs. Okoro: married couple
- Chidi: their biological son
- Dr. Talon: treating physician
- Scientist Obinna: laboratory professional responsible for analytical verification/validation
- Dr. Adewale: Medical Director/owner
