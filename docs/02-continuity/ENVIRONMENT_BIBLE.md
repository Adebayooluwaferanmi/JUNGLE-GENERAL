# Environment Bible — Human Canon

## Architectural Concept
JUNGLE GENERAL is a realistic Nigerian hospital presented with an established **jungle root-and-rock exterior identity** and believable modern clinical interiors. The environment is no longer an animal allegory.

# 1. Emergency Ward
**Status:** CANON

Established geography:
- central patient bed
- screen-left red trolley / shelving
- screen-right patient monitor, oxygen cylinder and blue curtain
- protected clinical interior remains dry
- storm/rain remains outside when storm continuity is active

Primary Episode 1 use: assessment and care of Mr. Okoro with Mrs. Okoro, Chidi and Dr. Talon.

# 2. Diagnostic Laboratory — Master
**Status:** CANON

This is the master environment that governs the laboratory's overall architecture and spatial identity. Sub-areas A/B/C must feel like coherent parts of this same laboratory rather than unrelated rooms.

# 3. A — Specimen Reception
**Status:** CANON

Function:
- specimen arrival/intake
- handoff into the laboratory workflow
- purple-top EDTA specimen continuity where applicable

# 4. B — Obinna Analytical Workstation
**Status:** CANON

Function:
- FBC/analyzer work
- review of questionable initial result
- analyzer maintenance/cleaning
- rerun/comparison workflow

This zone must support Obinna's deliberate technical work visually; avoid generic “scientist pressing random buttons” staging.

# 5. C — Result Validation
**Status:** CANON

Function:
- review/correlation
- validation/release
- communication-ready result state

Do not use any obsolete screen that incorrectly identifies Chidi as the patient.

# Environment Continuity Rules
1. Fixed architecture does not migrate between shots.
2. Equipment identity and placement should remain stable once approved.
3. Lab sub-areas must maintain a believable spatial relationship to the master environment.
4. Rain/wetness cannot appear inside protected clinical spaces without story cause.
5. Lighting changes require time/source motivation.
6. Use exact approved environment filenames once registered.
