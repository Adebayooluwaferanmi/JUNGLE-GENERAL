## Shot Backfill Procedure
For every recovered human shot:
1. preserve its actual source filename;
2. identify episode/sequence/scene;
3. assign stable shot ID;
4. record story beat;
5. record start state;
6. record camera position/axis;
7. record human blocking;
8. record end state;
9. record reference package;
10. classify CANDIDATE / REJECTED / APPROVED / LOCKED;
11. connect predecessor/successor continuity.
